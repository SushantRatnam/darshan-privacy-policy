Privacy Policy

Darshan - Hindu HD Wallpapers · Last updated: April 12, 2026

Overview

Darshan ("the App") is a free wallpaper application that provides Hindu-themed HD wallpapers. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. We do not require account creation, login, or any personally identifiable information to use the App.

The App may collect the following non-personal data:

  • Crash reports: We use Sentry to collect anonymous crash data (device model, OS version, stack traces) to improve app stability. No personal data is included.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Locally stored favorites: Your saved wallpapers are stored on your device only using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob - for displaying advertisements (Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry - for crash reporting (Privacy Policy)

Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Internet access: To load wallpapers and display ads.
  • Storage/Media Library: To save wallpapers to your device gallery.
  • Set Wallpaper: To apply wallpapers directly to your home or lock screen.

Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Crash reports are retained by Sentry for 90 days. Ad-related data is managed by Google per their retention policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

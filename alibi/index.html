Privacy policy for Alibi: Offline Party Game, an Android app by Srakk (package com.srakk.alibi).

Effective date: 17 October 2026

What the app does

Alibi is a pass-the-phone party game for people playing together in the same room. Gameplay, word packs, role assignment, timers, and scores work on the device without an account or internet connection.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share player names, clues, guesses, scores, or game history through the app. Alibi does not contain advertisements, analytics, accounts, in-app purchases, recording, or a backend service. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library.

Data stored on your device

Player names that you enter, game settings, scores, and your current game are stored locally so you can resume. A saved round includes the secret word-pair reference, role assignment, and any committed final guess. Unsubmitted guess text is not saved. This local storage is not encrypted. Secret-card visibility is not saved; returning to a deal starts with its word hidden. Android cloud backup is disabled for this release.

Deleting local data

In Alibi, open Privacy & support on the home screen and choose Clear saved game and names, then confirm. This resets saved names, settings, scores, and game progress on that device. You can also clear Alibi's storage in Android's app settings or uninstall the app. No account or server-side game profile exists for us to delete.

Support messages and external links

If you choose to email srakk@anglernook.com, your email provider delivers your message and email address to us. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of a support conversation by contacting the same address. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Opening this policy or an email link launches your browser or email app. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard web-request metadata when you visit. This does not enable analytics or collection of gameplay data in Alibi.

Audience

Alibi is designed for teens and adults playing a word and bluffing game together. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The effective date appears above. Developer: Srakk. For privacy questions or support, email srakk@anglernook.com.

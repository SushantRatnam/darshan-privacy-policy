Privacy policy for Alibi: Undercover Party Game, an Android app by Srakk (package com.srakk.alibi).

Effective date: 3 November 2026

What the app does

Alibi is a pass-the-phone party game for people playing together in the same room. Gameplay, word packs, role assignment, timers, and scores work on the device without an account or internet connection.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share player names, clues, guesses, scores, or game history through the app. Alibi does not contain advertisements, Alibi accounts, or recording. Releases through 1.2 do not send usage reports. Version 1.3 adds optional anonymous reports, off by default, as described below. Free gameplay does not use a gameplay backend. Optional Premium purchases, when enabled, use Google Play and a separate purchase-verification service described below. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo library.

Data stored on your device

Player names that you enter, custom word packs, your table theme, game settings (including difficulty and language-specific avoided word pairs), scores, and your current game are stored locally so you can resume. A saved round includes the secret word-pair reference, role assignment, and any committed final guess. Unsubmitted guess text is not saved. This local storage is not encrypted. Secret-card visibility is not saved; returning to a deal starts with its word hidden. Android cloud backup is disabled for this release.

Deleting local data

In Alibi, open Privacy & support on the home screen and choose Clear saved game and names, then confirm. This resets saved custom word packs, theme, names, settings, scores, and game progress on that device. You can also clear Alibi's storage in Android's app settings or uninstall the app. Avoided pairs can also be restored in Privacy & support without clearing your table. No account or server-side game profile exists for us to delete.

Optional usage reports and local diagnostics (1.3 and later)

Sharing is off by default. In Privacy & support you can choose Enable anonymous reports. Future events are then sent over HTTPS to an isolated Supabase service operated for Srakk. Reports contain only the app version and counts of app opens, returning opens, quick starts, tables and rounds started/completed, next-round and leave actions, practice actions, Premium screen/sample views, purchase/verification outcomes and fixed error categories (app, load, save or billing). They contain no names, words, clues, guesses, scores, raw error messages or stack traces, purchase tokens, advertising IDs, account details, location, or persistent device identifier. Local counters and the report-sharing preference are stored on your device; they do not include game content.

Counts help us understand broad usage and reliability. They do not identify individual people or prove individual-user retention or purchases. We store only daily totals by app version and event type, with automatic deletion of totals older than 90 days. No raw event stream or device profile is stored. Supabase and network providers may process standard connection metadata, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. Our transient rate limiter uses an IP address in memory and does not store it with the totals. Reports are not sold or used for advertising.

Turn reporting off at any time to cancel pending delivery and discard the memory-only batch. Events recorded before opt-in are not uploaded later. Offline reports can be lost when the app closes. Already-received totals cannot be traced back to you for individual deletion; they expire automatically. Local counters are removed by clearing Alibi app storage or uninstalling. The saved-game reset does not change your report-sharing preference. Gameplay works offline with sharing either on or off. Native crash and ANR information that Google makes available to developers through Android vitals is governed by Google's platform settings and policies.

Optional Premium purchases

Premium purchases are not enabled in the current setup build. When purchases become available, Google Play handles checkout and payment information under its own policies. Alibi does not receive your card or bank details. Our verification service, hosted on Supabase, receives the purchase token and product identifier to confirm purchase status, acknowledge a completed purchase, restore access, and check refunds or revocations. It stores the token, its hash, purchase state, acknowledgement and test-purchase flags, and verification/reconciliation timestamps in access-restricted server records. It does not receive player names, custom words, clues, guesses, or scores. Google may send purchase-status notifications to this service; notifications are checked against Google before updating access.

Purchase records are used to provide the paid feature and support ongoing restoration and purchase-status checks. They are retained while needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Contact srakk@anglernook.com for a purchase-data or deletion request; we may need to confirm ownership. Clearing local game data does not cancel, refund, or erase a Google Play purchase. Manage the purchase through Google Play. Connecting to Google Play and our service is required to verify or restore Premium on an app launch; gameplay can then continue offline in that running session, and an already-saved round remains finishable.

Support messages and external links

If you choose to email srakk@anglernook.com, your email provider delivers your message and email address to us. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of a support conversation by contacting the same address. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Opening this policy or an email link launches your browser or email app. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard web-request metadata when you visit. Visiting this website does not enable optional usage reports in Alibi.

Audience

Alibi is designed for teens and adults playing a word and bluffing game together. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact
